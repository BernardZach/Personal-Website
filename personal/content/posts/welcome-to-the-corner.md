# Welcome to the Corner

*March 12, 2026 • intro*

This section of my website is my indie web space. It is separate from my professional portfolio and exists for personal posts, experiments, and random internet fun.

I wanted a layout inspired by late 90s and early 2000s personal sites: tiled backgrounds, chunky UI boxes, and lots of personality.

Over time I’ll add build logs, game notes, dev diary entries, and links to stuff I’m currently obsessed with.

## What you'll find here

- Dev diary posts
- Indie game and music notes
- Small web experiments
- Changelog updates for this mini-site
